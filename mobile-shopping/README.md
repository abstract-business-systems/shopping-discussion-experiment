# Mobile Shopping

This folder contains the mobile-phone purchase research and a reusable purchase strategy.

## Structure

- `strategy/general-purchase-strategy.md` — social-proof-first process for future purchases.
- `purchases/mobile-phone/requirements.md` — requirements and trade-offs.
- `purchases/mobile-phone/research.md` — research notes and source links.
- `purchases/mobile-phone/evidence.tsv` — timestamped marketplace evidence snapshots, with separate seller/listing entries and a qualitative user-review summary column.
- `purchases/mobile-phone/mobile-phone-shopping-report.md` — current shortlist, budget tiers, and recommendation.
- `purchases/mobile-phone/decision-history.md` — chronological summary of how requirements and recommendations changed.

Marketplace ratings, review counts, prices, seller availability, and stock are snapshots. Revisit `evidence.tsv` in future sessions and append new rows rather than overwriting old evidence.
